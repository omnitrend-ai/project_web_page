What We Built

Trend Intelligence,
Reimagined

OmniTrend turns any topic into clear, trusted insights. Enter a keyword, get a full picture: timeline, sentiment, key players, and source bias. Every number traceable, every chart source-linked.

🧠

AI-Powered Trend Tracker

Our system leverages machine learning to detect and analyze patterns in real time, helping you stay ahead of market movements and social discussions.

📡

Catch What's Happening

Track live conversations and topics from verified news APIs, RSS feeds, and GDELT, identifying the stories people are engaging with most.

🕵️

Fake Trend Detection

Spot manipulated or artificial trends by analyzing abnormal spikes, bot activity, and irregular posting patterns across sources.

📊

Sentiment Analysis

Understand the emotions behind trends (positive, neutral, or negative) by measuring public sentiment and tone in real-time conversations.

🗺️

Regional Comparison

Compare how stories are framed across countries and outlets side-by-side. See top regions, key entities, and bias scores at a glance.

📤

Export Everything

Export charts as PNG, raw data as CSV, or generate a clean PDF summary, ready for presentations, research, or reporting.
